PUBLIC CATALOGS AND PLAYLISTS
Catalog content is downloaded at runtime; no current channel database is promised
by this package. Found M3U lists are stored locally with their source URL, fetched
time, optional Last-Modified and SHA-256. Download success is not playback proof.
Radio Browser: https://docs.radio-browser.info/
  https://de1.api.radio-browser.info/json/stations/search
  Mirrors: nl1.api.radio-browser.info and at1.api.radio-browser.info.
iptv-org: https://github.com/iptv-org/iptv and https://github.com/iptv-org/api
  https://iptv-org.github.io/iptv/index.m3u
  https://iptv-org.github.io/iptv/countries/{code}.m3u
  https://iptv-org.github.io/api/channels.json
  https://iptv-org.github.io/api/streams.json
  Mirrors use the same projects on raw.githubusercontent.com, not independent data.
Free-TV/IPTV: https://github.com/Free-TV/IPTV
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Free-TV/IPTV/master/playlist.m3u8
Bing public RSS: https://www.bing.com/search?format=rss&q={query}
Public broadcaster pages and supported media endpoints are requested at runtime.
The extractor is original application logic; Streamlink and a browser engine are
not bundled. Direct browser pages are excluded from the playable channel list.
Search availability, catalog accuracy and broadcaster access are not guaranteed.
Streams remain with their respective providers; the application does not host or
redistribute the media. Source metadata does not establish broadcasting rights.

EPG
Runtime defaults: https://iptv-epg.org/files/epg-{country}.xml.gz and
https://free-epg.de/api/epg/{country}.xml.gz
Provider pages: https://iptv-epg.org/guides and https://free-epg.de/
XMLTV is downloaded on demand; no programme database is bundled. Sources may
share underlying data. Users may choose a playlist-provided XMLTV URL or their
own source in EPG settings. Programme data does not provide stream access.
